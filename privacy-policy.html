Privacy Policy – LumiPDF

Last updated: 2 November 2026

LumiPDF ("the app") is a PDF editor, scanner and reader for Android. This policy explains what happens to your data.

Your documents stay on your device

Opening, editing, scanning, signing, merging, converting and saving PDFs all happen locally on your phone. We have no user accounts and we never receive your documents or files, with the one exception described below (free AI questions, text only, never the file itself).

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a document, an ID card / passport page, or a QR code or barcode. Photos and the text read from codes are processed on the device and are not uploaded by us.

Optional AI features

If you use the AI tab (summaries, questions, handwriting-style notes), the pages or text you choose are sent to the AI provider you select, using the API key you enter. The key is stored only on your device. That provider's own privacy policy applies to what you send. Nothing is sent unless you start an AI action.

Free AI questions (AI features, no key added)

If you use the AI features (AI Assistant, Translate, Summarize, Ask, handwriting-style notes) without adding your own AI key, a small number of free questions a day are answered through LumiPDF's own server (run on Cloudflare Workers), which holds the AI provider keys on your behalf. In that case the text of your question and the text of the pages you chose in the open PDF (extracted on your phone; never the PDF file itself) are sent to that server and from there to an AI provider to get an answer; the server does not store this text beyond what is needed to answer the question. A random identifier created on your device (not tied to your name, email or Google account) is sent with each question so the server can count how many you have used that day; it is stored on our server only as a daily count, and is deleted automatically after a few days. Watching a rewarded ad grants more free questions for the day; Google verifies with our server that the ad was watched in full, using the same advertising identifier described above. Adding your own AI key turns this off: your questions then go straight from your device to the provider you chose, as described above.

Microphone (voice commands)

The microphone is used only when you press the mic button in the AI Assistant. The recording is sent to the speech service you set up with your own key (Sarvam AI or Google Gemini) to turn it into text, and is not stored by us. Typing works without the microphone.

Digital signatures and certificates

If you use Digital Sign, the certificate file (.p12 / .pfx) you choose is read and used on your phone to sign the PDF. A copy of the certificate file (protected by its own password) can be kept inside the app on your phone so you can pick it again; it is never uploaded by us. The certificate password is not saved. Signed PDFs are saved on your phone, and the names and e-mail addresses typed in “Request signatures” are only remembered on your phone.

DocuSign and links

The DocuSign button prepares your PDF and hands it to the DocuSign app or website (or the share list) only when you press it; DocuSign's own privacy policy then applies. Links found in QR codes open only after you confirm.

Advertising

The free app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use the Android advertising ID and device information to show ads and measure them. In the EEA/UK and other regions where required, Google's consent form is shown first and no ads load without consent. You can change your choice any time in Help ▸ Support ▸ Ad Choices, or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can watch one optional rewarded video to hide ads for 24 hours. This pass is saved only on your device.

Data we collect

We (the developer) collect no personal data. Third parties named above (Google AdMob, and the AI provider you choose) may process data as described.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
